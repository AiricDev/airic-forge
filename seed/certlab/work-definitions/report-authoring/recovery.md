# Recovery

An interrupted response may hide a committed effect. Keep unknown outcomes unknown.
Resolve the original resource/scope/key, capability and exact target.
Never invent a new idempotency key or automatically rebase and repeat a write.
A missing receipt is not proof of failure; in-flight requests may still commit.
Archived/reincluded clauses have distinct Assessment identities. Old receipts remain old.
Stopping does not undo committed facts or accepted Jobs.
Report outstanding commands and remaining work for a human takeover.
